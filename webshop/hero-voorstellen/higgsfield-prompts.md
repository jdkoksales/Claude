# Prompts voor de gegenereerde hero's

Klaargezet om af te vuren zodra de beeldgenerator weer bereikbaar is.

- **Model:** `nano_banana_pro` — van de beschikbare modellen het sterkst in
  leesbare tekst, en het accepteert een referentiefoto.
- **Referentie:** de echte productfoto, al ingelezen als media
  `3f22071c-7d7f-4f8d-b197-4284bf7e3b7a`
  (`webshop/theme3/assets/tk3-prod-wit.jpg`).
- **Verhouding:** 16:9, resolutie 2k.

De tekst op het bordje moet kloppen; de QR mag afwijken. Daarom staat de
tekst in de prompt tussen aanhalingstekens en zo kort mogelijk — hoe minder
tekens, hoe kleiner de kans dat er een letter sneuvelt.

---

## 1 — Groot

> Photorealistic product hero photograph of the acrylic counter standee from
> the reference image, standing upright on a light stone reception counter.
> The standee is a small vertical panel on a solid base, about the height of
> a coffee cup, shown at a slight three-quarter angle so the base and the
> back support leg are clearly visible. The white panel reads
> "We'd love your feedback" at the top, then a row of five gold stars, then a
> square QR code, then "Tap or Scan" at the bottom. Warm orange gradient
> background, deep burnt orange at the top fading to light amber at the
> bottom. Soft studio lighting from the upper left, crisp contact shadow
> where the base meets the counter. Generous empty space in the upper half
> for a headline. Commercial product photography, sharp focus, no text
> anywhere except on the standee itself.

## 2 — Drieluik

> Photorealistic product photograph of three acrylic counter standees in a
> row on a light stone reception counter, evenly spaced, all standing upright
> on their bases with the back support legs visible. The left one is white,
> the middle one is magenta-to-pink gradient, the right one is cornflower
> blue. Each is a small vertical panel on a solid foot, coffee-cup height.
> Each panel shows a square QR code and the words "Tap or Scan" at the
> bottom. Warm orange gradient background, deep burnt orange at the top
> fading to light amber at the bottom. Soft studio lighting, crisp contact
> shadows under each base. Empty space in the upper third for a headline.
> Commercial product photography, sharp focus.

## 3 — Mechanisme

> Photorealistic product photograph: an acrylic counter standee stands
> upright on a light stone counter on the right side of the frame, at a
> slight angle so its base and back support leg are visible. A hand holds a
> modern smartphone close to the standee, as if tapping it. The white panel
> of the standee reads "We'd love your feedback" at the top, five gold stars
> below it, then a square QR code, then "Tap or Scan". Warm orange gradient
> background, deep burnt orange at the top fading to light amber at the
> bottom. Soft studio lighting from the upper left, crisp contact shadow
> under the base. The entire left half of the frame is empty background for
> a headline. Commercial product photography, shallow depth of field on the
> background, sharp focus on the standee.

---

## Waar op te letten bij de uitkomst

1. **Staat de voet erop?** Dat was de hele klacht bij de vorige ronde.
2. **Klopt de tekst letterlijk?** "We'd love your feedback" en "Tap or Scan"
   moeten er zonder verhaspelde letters staan.
3. **Is de linkerhelft (of bovenhelft) leeg genoeg** voor de koptekst?
4. Een afwijkende QR is akkoord — die scant toch niemand vanaf een hero.
